# Preparation pack

Keep the first pass concise enough to review together. Expand only the chosen approach.

1. **Assignment interpretation:** entity identities, role, exercise, time, output, explicit evaluation criteria and consequential unknowns.
2. **Company and industry brief:** only facts that change a choice. Include relevant executive direction and how it affects the role/customer argument.
3. **Private panel map:** actual role, assigned persona, evidence, likely scrutiny (inferred when appropriate), question to prepare for. Do not infer personality or private beliefs from a profile.
4. **POV choices and recommendation:** evidence → business consequence → buyer → supported product contribution → validation → next step. Distinguish a hypothesis from established customer pain.
5. **Candidate narrative:** motivation, relevant experience, personal action, result and lesson. Flag missing examples instead of filling them in.
6. **Timed meeting plan:** elapsed checkpoints, interaction prompts, time reserved for questions and close, interruption cut list. The durations must sum to the allotted time.
7. **Defense and pivot:** strongest objection, weakest assumption, evidence that would change the plan, and alternate path if the assumption fails.
8. **Evidence and open questions:** claim, status, source/date, implication, next validation action. Move unsupported factual assertions out of presentation copy; keep clearly framed hypotheses visible.

Include an explain-back invitation at a natural decision point: ask the candidate to state the main argument and weakest assumption in their own words. Don't turn every section into a quiz.

Private notes can explain what a sales choice demonstrates to the panel. Customer-facing copy should speak to the buyer, not narrate the candidate's interview strategy.

Redact or generalize confidential former-employer/customer information in shareable materials. Don't convert a precise confidential number into a fabricated public number; omit it or use an explicitly anonymized description approved by the candidate. A private link is not access control.
