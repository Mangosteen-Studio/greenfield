# Route by exercise

| Exercise | Focus | Useful output | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mock discovery / first call | Shared agenda, business problem, layered questions, listening, qualification, agreed next step | Short hypothesis, question branches, buyer role card, timed conversation | Product monologue, treating hypotheses as discovered pain |
| Named account plan | Wedge, stakeholder sequence, evidence, blockers, validation and milestones | Account plan with justified priorities and pivot | Invented relationships, calling a seller wish list a mutual plan |
| Territory / 30-60-90 | Learning, account prioritization, time allocation, pipeline assumptions, operating rhythm | Phased actions with dependencies and success checks | Arbitrary pipeline promises, identical 30-day buckets for every role |
| Executive pitch / case | Customer consequence, differentiated product fit, proof, decision request | Short narrative and assumption-led business case | Feature lists, guaranteed ROI, assuming budget authority from title |
| Product demo | Buyer task, supported workflow, discovery checks, business outcome | Verified demo path and fallback | Invented product UI or simulated functions presented as live |

Use a hybrid only when the assignment calls for it. For a 30-minute slot, a possible allocation is 3 minutes framing, 15 core exercise, 8 discussion, 4 next steps; adapt to whether Q&A is inside or outside the stated time. If duration is missing, label the proposed timing as provisional.

For planning math show inputs, units, calculation and uncertainty. A hypothetical deal size is an assumption, not employer data. Avoid treating activity volume as evidence of pipeline creation.
