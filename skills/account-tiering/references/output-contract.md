# Account Tiering Brief

Adapt depth to the list and the user's decision. For a large book, supply a compact executive view plus a sortable register rather than writing long prose for every row. A requested quick pass may be provisional; identify what it excludes.

## 1. Decision and scope

State the product/use case, account population, planning horizon, objectives and actual data access. Recommend where to focus, which customer obligations must be protected, and which uncertainty most affects the ranking. Distinguish completed coverage from sampled/unprocessed accounts.

## 2. Tiering contract

Show the tier meanings, hard disqualifiers, current-priority labels, confidence policy and any numerical model. Mark proposed assumptions. Preserve the user's existing tier field alongside recommended coverage when their meanings differ.

## 3. Account register

Minimum decision fields:

| Account ID / name | Lifecycle / workstreams | Fit / credible potential | Coverage tier | Current priority | Confidence and reason | Decisive evidence | Next action / owner | Change or review trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use additional fields or tables for currency/period, current spend, incremental whitespace, opportunity IDs, parent entity, source dates, effort, prior tier and missing data. Do not cram all these into unreadable cells. Monetary estimates must carry their basis and time horizon; unsupported values stay unknown.

## 4. Exceptions and data gaps

Show identity/duplicate candidates, assignment conflicts, hard exclusions and critical unknowns with owner and validation action. Report reconciliation counts from input rows to unique entities and reviewed accounts. These queues are part of the result, not hidden discarded rows.

## 5. Weekly coverage plan

| Account / workstream | Action and expected decision/output | Owner | Effort | Dependency / deadline | Why this week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Show available hours, mandatory allocations, discretionary allocations, explicit buffer, total and unallocated/overflow hours. If the capacity input is missing, label the result a ranked queue rather than a scheduled plan. Separate the next concrete task from a long-term coverage cadence. Do not create calendar events as part of this document.

## 6. Validation and maintenance

List the few missing facts most likely to change a tier, their evidence source and research owner. Include promotion/demotion conditions, review dates where justified, and a short change log on refresh. Keep manager overrides and their cost visible.

## 7. Next workflow

Route selected accounts to Greenfield, Account Expansion, Co-Selling, POV or QBR if available and useful. End with one immediate action and unresolved limitations. A tiering recommendation is not outreach authorization, confirmed pipeline or proof of buying intent.
