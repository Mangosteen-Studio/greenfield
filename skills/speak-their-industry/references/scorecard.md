# Practice scorecard

Score each area 1–5 from the transcript. Quote the seller's words for every score. If an area did not come up, mark it "not tested" rather than scoring it.

| Area | 1 (outsider) | 3 (competent) | 5 (sounds like they've sold here before) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Industry language** | Generic sales or tech jargon; misuses industry terms | Uses a few terms correctly | Uses the buyer's terms naturally and correctly; translates product features into the buyer's metrics |
| **Buyer's priorities** | Pitches features before understanding the buyer | Asks about priorities but stays general | Ties questions to what this role is measured on and the pressure they mentioned |
| **Rules and approvals** | Ignores regulation and approval steps, or overclaims compliance | Acknowledges them when the buyer raises them | Raises the relevant review or requirement early, asks how it works here, and does not overclaim |
| **Objection handling** | Argues, deflects or makes unsupported claims | Responds reasonably but generically | Acknowledges the concern, asks a clarifying question, and answers with specific, honest evidence |
| **Discovery** | Few questions, or questions the buyer could not answer | Some good questions | Uncovers the hidden detail; asks about process, people, timing and past attempts |
| **Next step** | No ask, or a vague "let's stay in touch" | Asks for a next step without specifics | Proposes one specific next step with who, what and when, matched to how this buyer buys |

## Output

1. **Scores:** the table with a score and a quote for each area.
2. **What worked:** two moments to repeat, quoted.
3. **Three changes:** the three changes that would most improve the next run, each with a rewritten line the seller could have said.
4. **Credibility risks:** any claim the seller made that could damage trust in a real meeting (overclaimed compliance, invented customers, wrong terminology).
5. **Next run:** the recommended difficulty variant.

Be direct and specific. Do not inflate scores to be encouraging.
