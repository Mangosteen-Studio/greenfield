# Practice scorecard

Score each area 1–5 using only what is observable in the transcript. Quote the seller's words for every score. For a missed opportunity, quote the buyer's cue and the seller's response. If an area did not come up, mark it "not tested" rather than scoring it.

Before scoring:
- If speaker labels are missing or unclear, infer them carefully and say that you did.
- Voice transcription often garbles terms such as BAA, FedRAMP or KYC. Treat an odd term as a possible transcription error unless the context makes the misuse clear.
- If part of the conversation is missing, score what is there and say what is missing. Do not reconstruct it.

| Area | 1 (outsider) | 3 (competent) | 5 (sounds like they've sold here before) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opener and industry language** | Generic sales or tech jargon; misuses industry terms | Uses a few terms correctly | Opens in the buyer's terms and translates features into the buyer's metrics. Jargon used for its own sake does not score higher |
| **Buyer's priorities** | Pitches features before understanding the buyer | Asks about priorities but stays general | Ties questions to what this role is measured on and what the buyer said |
| **Rules and approvals** | Ignores regulation and approval steps, or overclaims compliance | Acknowledges them when the buyer raises them | Raises the relevant review or requirement, asks how it works here, and does not overclaim |
| **Objection handling** | Argues, deflects or makes unsupported claims | Responds reasonably but generically | Acknowledges the concern, asks a clarifying question, and answers with specific, honest evidence |
| **Discovery and listening** | Few questions, or questions the buyer could not answer | Some good questions | Follows up on the buyer's answers; asks about process, people, timing and past attempts. If the buyer revealed the secret detail at the end, note whether the seller uncovered it |
| **Next step** | No ask, or a vague "let's stay in touch" | Asks for a next step without specifics | Proposes one specific next step with who, what and when, matched to how this buyer buys |

## Output

1. **Scores:** the table with a score and a quote for each area.
2. **What worked:** two moments to repeat, quoted.
3. **Three changes:** the changes that would most improve the next run, each with a rewritten line the seller could have said.
4. **Credibility risks:** any claim that could damage trust in a real meeting, such as overclaimed compliance, an unverified customer claim or wrong terminology. Call unsupported claims "unverified", not invented.
5. **Next run:** one suggested variant from the roleplay template.

Be direct and specific. Do not inflate scores to be encouraging.
