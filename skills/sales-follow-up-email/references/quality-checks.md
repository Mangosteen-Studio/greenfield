# Quality checks

Run these before returning any email.

## Evidence check

- Every priority, problem, number, date, name and commitment appears in the call notes or transcript.
- Anything uncertain is marked `[confirm: …]`, not guessed.
- Nothing from the seller's private notes (deal assessment, competitor views, forecast) appears in buyer-facing text.

## Sales slop detector

Flag and remove:
- “Just following up”, “circling back”, “touching base”, “bumping this to the top of your inbox”.
- “Hope this email finds you well” and other filler openers.
- “Per our conversation” followed by a full transcript-style recap.
- Feature lists or product pitching the buyer did not ask about.
- Generic statistics or value claims not tied to the buyer's situation.
- Fake urgency: invented deadlines, expiring discounts, “last chance”.
- Guilt or pressure: “I haven't heard back”, “I've tried to reach you several times”.
- More than one ask, or an ask with no date.
- Ending with “Let me know if you have any questions” instead of a next step.

## Shape check

- Main recap is under about 150 words.
- Subject line is specific: company, topic or next step.
- The next step names what, who and when.
- The buyer can read it in under a minute on a phone.

If the email fails the evidence check, fix it or return `NO EMAIL YET` with what needs confirming. Do not return a polished email built on invented details.
